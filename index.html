    Connect your agent

    The console runs here, but messages to your switch are sent by a small agent on a PC inside your network. Your switch IPs, accounts and test evidence stay on that PC.

    1. Download the agent
      Needs Java 8 or later and your FLEXCUBE switch simulator (the agent uses your own licensed copy).
    2. Point it at your simulator. Unzip it and set simulatorHome in agent.properties to your simulator's version folder, e.g. …\Simulator\Version_87.
    3. Start it with start.bat (Windows) or start.sh. It opens this page already paired.
