      Cutover rehearsal

      ?—

      The card switch runs in stand-in while no core is connected, then its queue goes to the New Flexcube Core. SwitchProof plays the switch through each step and reconciles every transaction, account and GL.

      1. 1Switch to stand-in
        Stand-in simulations are part of the Enterprise plan.
      2. 2Positive balance file

        The balances the switch decides on: the Legacy Core's at the cut-off. A CSV with one account a line: account,available_balance[,currency] · template

      3. 3Transactions at the switch

        No core is connected: the switch approves or declines each one on its limits and the balance file, and queues what it approved.

        Authorizations

        A hold: clearing debits the final amount and releases the hold.

        From before the cut-off, and force posting

        They come off the balance file first, as the switch saw them: load the file with the balances before them (the migrated balances, without holds).

      4. 4Connect the New Flexcube Core

        A sign-on (0800) to —. Nothing else is sent yet.

      5. 5Samples

        Balance inquiries show whether the migrated balances match the file; the first advices show the queue posts.

      6. 6Release the queue

      7. 7Reconcile

        Runs by itself after the release: every RRN, each account (before + approved in stand-in = after) and the switch's totals against the GLs.

      8. 8Fully online

        The switch leaves stand-in and live transactions go to the New Flexcube Core. Balance inquiries check what customers now see, and the switch's balance file is compared with the New Flexcube Core before it's refreshed.

      9. 9Clearing

        After go-live the authorizations still open are cleared: POS for the authorized amount or a different one (tips, partial shipments), cash as dispensed.

        Load the clearing list into the New Flexcube Core the way the bank loads its clearing, then check: SwitchProof reconciles every account against it.

          SwitchProof

          ISO 8583 · Oracle FLEXCUBE ATM & POS switch

          Functional, UAT and load testing for your card switch.

          Send real ISO 8583 traffic to the switch, check every response against what you expect, and keep evidence of each result for sign‑off.

          Functional
          Withdrawal, balance, purchase and settlement, transfer, refund, deposit and reversal, driven by CSV or Excel data.
          UAT
          Test-case library and scenario chains with expected response codes, pass/fail, printable evidence and your bank's test-sheet format.
          Load
          Hundreds of concurrent transactions with live throughput, latency and response-code breakdown.
          Environments
          UAT and production switches side by side, each with its own acquirer, terminal, card and test accounts.
          Data residency
          Messages are sent by a Windows agent inside your network. Switch addresses, accounts and evidence never reach this site.

          SwitchProof is a product of TrustRoute Technologies. For use with the Oracle FLEXCUBE ATM/POS switch. Not affiliated with Oracle; Oracle and FLEXCUBE are trademarks of Oracle Corporation.

                  1.5.0
                  11 November 2026Any switch format, honest numbers, a simulator and a command line
                  
        
                  Message formats Team
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Describe your switch's format without writing a file: Target, then New format. Pick the text encoding (ASCII or EBCDIC), packed or text numbers, the bitmap, the message length header, and any header your switch adds.
                    • 
          
                  • Paste a message captured from your switch and SwitchProof reads it field by field with your format, showing exactly where it stops if something doesn't fit.
                    • 
          
                  • New field kinds for packed numbers and track data, and chip data (field 55) shown tag by tag.
                    • 
          
                  • Text fields now accept every printable character, such as the slash and comma in a merchant name.
                    • 
          
                  • A help page explains each format setting, with examples: How formats work, under the message format in Target.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Load tests
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Results now show how long half, 95% and 99% of answers took, and the slowest, not only the average.
                    • 
          
                  • Ramp-up: let the rate climb to full over the first seconds of a run.
                    • 
          
                  • If SwitchProof itself could not keep the pace you asked for, the results say so and suggest how many to run at once, so a slow tool is never mistaken for a slow switch.
                    • 
          
                  • The chart follows the whole run second by second, and long runs no longer grow in memory.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Connection to the switch
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Kept-open connections: messages share connections that stay open, as an acquirer's link does, with replies matched by trace number and an echo test when the line is quiet.
                    • 
          
                  • TLS, with your switch's certificate pinned by its fingerprint or trusted through your PC.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Security on messages
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Send an encrypted PIN block (ISO formats 0, 1 and 3), chip data, and a MAC (X9.19, X9.9 or triple-DES CBC) with every card request: Target settings, Security.
                    • 
          
                  • Keys are test keys you paste once. SwitchProof shows only their check value afterwards and protects them with your Windows account.
                    • 
          
                  • The chip cryptogram is a placeholder unless you supply one, and MACs on replies are not checked yet.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Simulator Team
                  
        
                    
          
                  • New Simulator tab: SwitchProof answers as the switch or issuer, so you can test an ATM, a POS or your own switch's link without the real host.
                    • 
          
                  • Rules decide the answer by message type, processing code, card range, account or amount: a response code, a delay, or no reply at all. Everything else is approved.
                    • 
          
                  • It can keep balances, so withdrawals, holds, reversals and insufficient funds behave as they would on a host.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Money check without a database Enterprise
                  
        
                    
          
                  • UAT can now check the money side by balance inquiry before and after each step, for switches where you have no access to the core database.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Command line
                  
        
                    
          
                  • switchproof-cli runs UAT cases and load tests from a build pipeline, writes JUnit results, and ends with an exit code your pipeline can act on. It is installed next to SwitchProof.
                    • 
          
                  • A Linux bundle (needs Java 8 or later) is available on request.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Help and guide
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Every screen and setting explained, with examples and what to do when something goes wrong: Help and guide in the account menu, or the ? beside each screen and dialog.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Also
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Saved database passwords are now protected by your Windows account instead of being kept as plain text. Existing ones are converted the first time SwitchProof starts.
                    • 
          
                  • The connect screen works with Chrome's new local network permission.
                    •

                  8 November 2026Switch setup: customize your switch
                  
        
                  Switch setup Enterprise
                  
        
                    
          
                  • New Switch setup tab: SwitchProof reads how your FLEXCUBE switch interface handles each processing code (literal, product, accounting, holds and charges) from your bank's own database, read-only.
                    • 
          
                  • Customize a transaction in four steps: pick the code, choose what happens at each stage of its life cycle (authorization, advices, completion, reversals, repeats, failed postings, hold expiry), review, then download a change pack.
                    • 
          
                  • Product charges, your decline fee and the hold expiry can be changed in the same flow.
                    •

Only the points your switch actually calls are offered.
                    • 
          
                  • A change pack is a set of scripts for your DBA: backups first, the change, checks afterwards, and a rollback. SwitchProof never runs them. Your packages change only where the rules go; everything else stays as it is.
                    • 
          
                  • Works with any schema name, or with the read-only user's synonyms. A bank that doesn't connect SwitchProof to its database can upload table exports and package files instead.

                  1.4.6
                  12 October 2026Authorizations and clearing in stand-in
                  
        
                  Stand-in simulation Enterprise
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Authorizations the Legacy Core approved before the cut-off: when the migration brings balances without their holds, the switch replays them to the New Flexcube Core as 0120s, first in the queue and in time order.
                    • 
          
                  • ATM withdrawals can run as authorizations: 0100 at the switch, replayed as 0120.
                    • 
          
                  • Choose how the New Flexcube Core books a 0120: as a hold, or as a debit. The reconciliation checks each authorization the way you chose.
                    • 
          
                  • New step 9, Clearing: after go-live the open authorizations clear, POS for the authorized amount or a different one, cash as dispensed. Clearing goes as 0220 advices from the switch, or as a list the bank loads; every account is then reconciled.
                    • 
          
                  • With debit booking the check expects clearing to post only the difference: nothing at the same amount, a further debit when it's more, a credit when it's less. Debiting the full amount again is flagged.
                    • 
          
                  • Going fully online now compares the switch's balance file with the New Flexcube Core before it's refreshed, account by account.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Also
                  
        
                    
          
                  • When a switch closes every connection without a reply, the load test says it isn't a concurrency problem and points at the network path, such as a load balancer that isn't passing plain TCP or an address that isn't allowed.

                  1 October 2026Your own types, sources and currencies
                  
        
                  Your own transaction types Team
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Design a transaction type from a built-in one: its own name, message class (x100, x120, x200, x220), processing code and fields. Add, change or leave out any field; values can use placeholders such as {random:8}, {date:MMdd}, {account} and {amount}.
                    • 
          
                  • A live preview shows the exact message for the active switch, field by field, and flags values that don't fit the field. Test send one message before you save.
                    • 
          
                  • Saved types work straight away in load tests, bundles and UAT cases. Money moves as in the type they started from, so reversals, settlements and money checks work the same.
                    • 
          
                  • Submit a type for approval to have it shipped as a built-in type for everyone. Only its structure is sent; fixed values are masked unless you choose to share them.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Sources: where a transaction comes from
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Ready-made sources per switch: on-us, remote on-us, off-us, international and our card abroad. Each sets the card (F2), acquirer (F32) and, if needed, forwarding institution, terminal, merchant type (F18) and entry mode (F22).
                    • 
          
                  • Pick a source for a run, for each bundle row or for each UAT step. Results and reports are grouped by source.
                    • 
          
                  • The money check compares the switch's on-us / off-us decision with the source, so a card or BIN set up wrongly on the switch is caught.
                    • 
        
                  
        
                  Currencies
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Settlement amount and currency with their rate (F5, F50, F9) and cardholder billing amount and currency with their rate (F6, F51, F10), set per source.
                    • 
          
                  • The money check understands converted postings: it shows the rate the core used, compares the debit with the billed amount, and flags a posting that wasn't converted at all.

                  1.4.3
                  27 September 2026Stand-in simulation
                  
        
                  Core banking cutover rehearsal Enterprise
                  
        
                    
          
                  • Eight steps, each a button as on the night: switch to stand-in, positive balance file, transactions at the switch, connect the new core, samples, release the queue, reconcile, fully online.
                    • 
          
                  • The simulated switch approves or declines in stand-in on its own rules and the positive balance file (51 not enough balance, 61 over a stand-in limit, 91 no balance record), and queues what it approved.
                    • 
          
                  • The queue is released as advices (0220, 0120, 0420) with the repeats a switch sends when it gets no answer (0221, 0121, 0421), in time order and without overtaking.
                    • 
          
                  • Transactions that began in the legacy core: reversals of withdrawals approved just before the cut-off, and completions of holds the migration brought over. A force-post test sends a withdrawal above the balance.
                    •
