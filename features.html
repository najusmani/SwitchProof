Load testing

ISO 8583 load tests

Send hundreds or thousands of transactions at the pace you set, and see how your switch keeps up.

  • Messages per second, and the time half, 95% and 99% of answers took, not just an average
  • A ramp-up, and how many messages wait for their reply at once

UAT

UAT test cases with printable evidence

Write each scenario once, run it on any environment, and hand over evidence your bank can sign off.

  • Chains of steps: purchase, settle, withdraw, reverse, transfer, balance
  • The answer you expect for each step: 00, a list such as 05,51, or any decline
  • Account roles, so the same cases run on UAT and production

Questions

Does SwitchProof see our card data?

No. SwitchProof for Windows sends the messages from your PC to your switch and keeps the results there. This website handles your account and plan only. Use test cards and test keys: SwitchProof is not an HSM.

Which switches does it work with?

Switches that speak ISO 8583 over TCP or TLS. Four formats are built in, including the Oracle FLEXCUBE switch interface; for another switch, describe its format in the console and check it against a message captured from it.

What do we need to install?

SwitchProof for Windows, on a PC that can reach your switch (bank network or VPN). It's about 35 MB with Java included, and needs no admin rights.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan runs load tests of up to 200 messages and UAT runs of up to 10 cases, with no card needed. Pricing lists Team and Enterprise.

Can it run in our CI pipeline?

Yes. switchproof-cli runs UAT cases and load tests, writes JUnit XML, and ends with an exit code your pipeline can act on. See the command line help.
